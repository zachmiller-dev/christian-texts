# Question 41

**But since that Christ died for us, why must we also die?**

Our death is not a satisfaction for our Sins, but the abolishing of Sin, and our passage¹ into everlasting Life.

## Scripture proofs

1. John 5:24; 1 Philippians 23; Romans 7:24
