# Question 37

**For what cause should he suffer under Pilate, as being his Judg?**

That he being innocent¹ and condemned before a civil Judg² might deliver us from the severe Judgment of God which remained for all men.

## Scripture proofs

1. Luke 23:14; John 19:4
2. Psalm 69:4; Isaiah 53:4–5; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Galatians 3:13
