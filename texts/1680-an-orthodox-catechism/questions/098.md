# Question 98

**What is the mortifying of the old Man?**

To be truly and heartily sorry that thou hast offended God by thy Sins, and daily more and more hate¹ and eschew them.

## Scripture proofs

1. Romans 8:13; Joel 2:13; Hosea 5:5
