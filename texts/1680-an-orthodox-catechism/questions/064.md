# Question 64

**Seeing then that only Faith maketh us Partakers of Christ and his Benefits, whence doth it proceed?**

From the Holy Ghost¹ who kindleth it in our Hearts by the preaching of the Gospel, and other Ordinances, and² confirmeth it by the use of the Sacraments.

## Scripture proofs

1. Ephesians 2:8; Ephesians 6:23; John 3:5; Philippians 1:29
2. Matthew 28:19–20; 1 Peter 1:22–23
