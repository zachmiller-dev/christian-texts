# Question 97

**Of what Parts consisteth the Conversion of Man unto God?**

It consisteth of the¹ mortifying of the old Man, and a quickning of the new Man.

## Scripture proofs

1. Romans 6:4–6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Colossians 3:5,8–10; 1 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 7:11
