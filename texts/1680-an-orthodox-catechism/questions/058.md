# Question 58

**But when thou believest all these things, what profit redoundeth thence unto thee?**

That I am righteous in Christ before God, and an Heir¹ of eternal Life.

## Scripture proofs

1. Romans 1:17; John 3:36; Romans 3:4,22,24–25,28; Romans 5:1; Galatians 2:16; Ephesians 2:8–9
