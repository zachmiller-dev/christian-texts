# Question 45

**How understand you that he ascended into Heaven?**

That Christ, his Disciples looking on, was¹ taken up from the Earth into Heaven, and yet² still is there for our sakes, and will be until he come again to judge the quick and³ the dead.

## Scripture proofs

1. Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:51
2. Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 9:11; Romans 8:34; Ephesians 4:10; Colossians 3:1
3. Acts 1:11; Matthew 24:30
