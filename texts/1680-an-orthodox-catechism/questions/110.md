# Question 110

**What doth this second Commandment require?**

That we should not¹ express or represent God by any Image or shape and figure, or worship him any otherwise then he hath commanded himself in² his Word to be worshipped.

## Scripture proofs

1. Deuteronomy 4:15,&c; Isaiah 40:18,&c; Romans 1:23,&c; Acts 17:29
2. 1 Samuel 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:30,&c; Matthew 15:9
