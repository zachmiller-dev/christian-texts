# Question 49

**Why is it further said, he sitteth at the Right-Hand of God?**

Because Christ therefore is ascended into Heaven, to¹ shew there that he is the Head of his Church, by whom the Father² governeth all things.

## Scripture proofs

1. Ephesians 1:20; Ephesians 5:23; Colossians 1:18
2. Matthew 28:18; John 5:22
