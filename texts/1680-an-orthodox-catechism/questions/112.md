# Question 112

**But may not Images be tolerated in Churches, which may serve for Books unto the common People?**

No, for ’tis not seemly that we should be wiser then God, who will have his Church to¹ be taught by the lively preaching of his Word, and not with² dumb Images.

## Scripture proofs

1. 2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:19
2. Jeremiah 10:8,&c; Habakkuk 2:18–19
