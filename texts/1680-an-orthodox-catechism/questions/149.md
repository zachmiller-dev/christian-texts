# Question 149

**What is the fifth Petition?**

Forgive us our Trespaffes, as we forgive them that trespass against us. That is, even for the Blood of Christ do not¹ impute unto us most miserable and wretched sinners all our Offences, neither that Corruption which still cleaveth unto us, even as we also feel this Testimony of thy Grace in our Hearts, that we stedfastly purpose unfeignedly from² our Hearts, to pardon and forgive all those who have offended us.

## Scripture proofs

1. Psalm 51:1; Psalm 143:2
2. Matthew 6:14
