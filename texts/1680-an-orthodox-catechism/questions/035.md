# Question 35

**What profit takest thou by Christ’s holy Conception and Nativity?**

That he is our¹ Mediator, and doth cover with his Innocency and perfect Holiness my Sins, in which I was conceived, that they may not come in the Sight of² God.

## Scripture proofs

1. Hebrews 2:16–17
2. Psalm 32:1; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Romans 8:3–4; Galatians 4:4–5
