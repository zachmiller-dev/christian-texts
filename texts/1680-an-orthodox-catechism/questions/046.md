# Question 46

**Is not Christ with us then until the end of the World, as he hath promised?**

¹ Christ is true God, and true Man, and so according to his Manhood is not now on Earth, but according to his Godhead, his Majesty, his Grace, and² Spirit is at no time from us.

## Scripture proofs

1. Matthew 28:20; Matthew 26:11; John 16:18; John 17:11; Acts 3:21
2. John 14:17,&c; John 16:13; Ephesians 4:8
