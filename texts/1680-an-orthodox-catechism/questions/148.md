# Question 148

**What is the fourth Petition?**

Give us this Day our daily bread; that is, give unto us all things which¹ are needful for this Life, that by them we may acknowledg and confess thee to be the only Fountain from whence all good things² flow; and all our Care and Industry, and even thine own Gifts to be unfortunate³ and noisom unto us except thou bless them; wherefore grant that turning our Trust away from all Creatures, we⁴ place and repose it in thee alone.

## Scripture proofs

1. Psalm 145:15–16; Psalm 10:4; Matthew 6:25,&c
2. Acts 17:27–28; Acts 14:16–17
3. 1 Corinthians 15:58; Deuteronomy 8:3; Psalm 27:13
4. Psalm 62:11
