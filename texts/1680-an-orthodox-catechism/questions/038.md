# Question 38

**But is there any more in it, that he was fastened to the Cross, than if he had suffered any other kind of death?**

There is more: for by this I am assured that he took upon himself the curse which did ly on me; for the death of the Cross¹ was accursed of God.

## Scripture proofs

1. Deuteronomy 21:23; Galatians 3:13
