# Question 32

**For what cause is Christ called the only begotten Son of God, when we also are the Sons of God?**

Because Christ alone is the eternal and natural Son of the¹ eternal Father, and² we are but Sons adopted of the Father by Grace for his sake.

## Scripture proofs

1. Hebrews 1:2–3; John 3:16
2. Romans 8:3; Ephesians 1:6; John 1:12; 1 John 1:3
