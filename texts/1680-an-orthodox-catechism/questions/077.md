# Question 77

**Is then the outward Baptism of Water the washing away of Sins?**

It is not; for the¹ Blood of Christ alone cleanseth us from² all Sin.

## Scripture proofs

1. 1 Peter 3:21; Ephesians 5:25–26
2. 1 John 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:11
