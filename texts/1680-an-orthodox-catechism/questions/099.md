# Question 99

**What is the quickning of the new Man?**

True Joy in God¹ through Christ, and an earnest Desire to order thy Life according to God’s Will and to² do all good Works.

## Scripture proofs

1. Romans 5:1; Romans 14:17; Isaiah 57:15
2. Romans 6:10–11; Galatians 2:20
