# Question 9

**Doth not God then Injury to Man who in the Law requireth that of him which he is not able to perform?**

No; For God had made Man such a one as¹ he might perform it: but Man by the impulsion of the² Devil, and his own stubbornness bereaved himself and all his posterity of those Divine Graces.

## Scripture proofs

1. John 3:5; Ephesians 4:24
2. Luke 10:30
