# Question 140

**What are those things which he commandeth us to ask of him?**

All things¹ necessary both for Soul and Body, which our Lord Jesus Christ hath comprised in that Prayer, which himself hath taught us.

## Scripture proofs

1. Matthew 6:13; James 1:17
