# Question 96

**Cannot they then be saved which be unthankful, and remain still careless in their Sins, and are not converted from their Wickedness to God?**

By no means; for as the Scripture beareth Witness, neither unchast Persons, nor Idolaters, nor Adulterers, nor Thievs, nor Covetous nor Drunkards, nor Slanderers, nor Robbers,¹ shall enter in to the Kingdom of God.

## Scripture proofs

1. 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:5–6; 1 John 3:14–15
