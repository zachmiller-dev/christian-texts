# A Catechism for Boys and Girls

Errol Hulse’s children’s catechism, Chapel Library edition (1998). Questions and answers in the booklet; Scripture proofs collected in a final section.

---

## God, Man, and Sin

### Question 1 — Who made you?

**Answer:** God made me.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 1:26–27
- Genesis 2:7
- Ecclesiastes 12:1
- Acts 17:24–29

### Question 2 — What else did God make?

**Answer:** God made *all* things.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 1, esp. vv. 1 and 3
- Acts 14:15
- Romans 11:36
- Colossians 1:16

### Question 3 — Why did God make you and all things?

**Answer:** For His own glory.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 19:1
- Jeremiah 9:23–24
- Revelation 4:11
- Revelation 5:13

### Question 4 — How can you glorify God?

**Answer:** By loving Him and doing what He commands.

*Proofs:*
- Ecclesiastes 12:13
- Mark 12:29–31
- John 15:8–10
- 1 Corinthians 10:31

### Question 5 — Why ought you to glorify God?

**Answer:** Because He made me and takes care of me.

*Proofs:*
- Romans 11:36
- Revelation 4:11
- Daniel 5:23

### Question 6 — Are there more gods than one?

**Answer:** There is only one God.

*Proofs:*
- Deuteronomy 6:4
- Jeremiah 10:10
- Mark 12:29
- Acts 17:22–31

### Question 7 — In how many Persons does this one God exist?

**Answer:** In three Persons.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 3:16–17
- John 5:23
- John 10:30
- John 14:9, 10
- John 15:26
- John 16:13–15
- 1 John 5:20
- 2 John 9
- Revelation 1:4–5

### Question 8 — Who are they?

**Answer:** The Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 28:19
- 2 Corinthians 13:14
- 1 Peter 1:2
- Jude 20–21

### Question 9 — Who is God?

**Answer:** God is a Spirit, and has not a body like men.

*Proofs:*
- John 4:24
- 2 Corinthians 3:17
- 1 Timothy 1:17

### Question 10 — Where is God?

**Answer:** God is everywhere.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 139:7–12
- Jeremiah 23:23–24
- Acts 17:27–28

### Question 11 — Can you see God?

**Answer:** No. I cannot see God, but He always sees me.

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 33:20
- John 1:18
- 1 Timothy 6:16
- Psalms 139, esp. vv. 1–5
- Proverbs 5:21
- Hebrews 4:12–13

### Question 12 — Does God know all things?

**Answer:** Yes. Nothing can be hidden from God.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Chronicles 28:9
- 2 Chronicles 16:9
- Luke 12:6–7
- Romans 2:16

### Question 13 — Can God do all things?

**Answer:** Yes. God can do all His holy will.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 147:5
- Jeremiah 32:17
- Daniel 4:34–35
- Ephesians 1:11

### Question 14 — Where do you learn how to love and obey God?

**Answer:** In the Bible alone.

*Proofs:*
- Job 11:7
- Psalms 119:104
- Isaiah 8:20
- Matthew 22:29
- 2 Timothy 3:15–17

### Question 15 — Who wrote the Bible?

**Answer:** Holy men who were taught by the Holy Spirit.

*Proofs:*
- 2 Peter 1:20–21
- Acts 1:16
- 2 Timothy 3:16
- 1 Peter 1:10–11

### Question 16 — Who were our first parents?

**Answer:** Adam and Eve.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 2:18–25
- Genesis 3:20
- Genesis 5:1–2
- Acts 17:26
- 1 Timothy 2:13

### Question 17 — Of what were our first parents made?

**Answer:** God made the body of Adam out of the ground, and formed Eve from the body of Adam.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 2:7
- Genesis 21–23
- Genesis 3:19
- Psalms 103:14

### Question 18 — What did God give Adam and Eve besides bodies?

**Answer:** He gave them souls that could never die.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Corinthians 15:45
- Ecclesiastes 12:7
- Zechariah 12:1

### Question 19 — Have you a soul as well as a body?

**Answer:** Yes. I have a soul that can never die.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 10:28
- Mark 8:34–38
- Mark 12:30

### Question 20 — How do you know that you have a soul?

**Answer:** Because the Bible tells me so.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 10:28
- Mark 8:34–38
- Mark 12:30

### Question 21 — In what condition did God make Adam and Eve?

**Answer:** He made them holy and happy.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 1:26–28
- Psalms 8:4–8

### Question 22 — Did Adam and Eve stay holy and happy?

**Answer:** No. They sinned against God.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 3:1–7
- Ecclesiastes 7:29
- Hosea 6:7 (“men”=Adam)

### Question 23 — What is sin?

**Answer:** Sin is any transgression of the Law of God.

*Proofs:*
- 1 John 3:4
- Romans 3:20
- James 2:9–11

### Question 24 — What is meant by transgression?

**Answer:** Doing what God forbids.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Samuel 13:8–14
- 1 Samuel 15:22–23
- Hosea 6:7
- Romans 1:21–32

### Question 25 — What was the sin of our first parents?

**Answer:** Eating the forbidden fruit.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 2:16–17
- Genesis 3:6

### Question 26 — Why did they eat the forbidden fruit?

**Answer:** Because they did not believe what God had said.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 3:1–6
- compare Hebrews 11:6

### Question 27 — Who tempted them to this sin?

**Answer:** The devil tempted Eve, and she gave the fruit to Adam.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 3:1–13
- 2 Corinthians 11:3
- 1 Timothy 2:13–14
- compare Revelation 12:9

### Question 28 — What happened to our first parents when they had sinned?

**Answer:** Instead of being holy and happy, they became sinful and miserable.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 3:14–24
- Genesis 4:1–24
- James 1:14–15

### Question 29 — What effect had the sin of Adam on all mankind?

**Answer:** All mankind is born in a state of sin and misery.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 51:5
- Romans 5:12, 18–19
- 1 Corinthians 15:21–22
- 1 John 5:19

### Question 30 — What do we inherit from Adam as a result of this original sin?

**Answer:** A sinful nature.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Kings 8:46
- Psalms 14:2, 3
- Psalms 58:3
- Ecclesiastes 9:3
- Matthew 15:18–20
- John 2:24–25
- Romans 8:7

### Question 31 — What does every sin deserve?

**Answer:** The anger and judgment of God.

*Proofs:*
- Deuteronomy 27:26
- Romans 1:18
- Romans 2:2
- Galatians 3:10
- Ephesians 5:6

### Question 32 — Can anyone go to heaven with this sinful nature?

**Answer:** No. Our hearts must be changed before we can be fit for heaven.

*Proofs:*
- Jeremiah 31:33–34
- Ezekiel 36:25–27
- John 1:12–13
- John 3:1–10
- 1 John 5:1, 4, 18

### Question 33 — What is a change of heart called?

**Answer:** Regeneration.

*Proofs:*
- Titus 3:5–7

### Question 34 — Who can change a sinner’s heart?

**Answer:** The Holy Spirit alone.

*Proofs:*
- John 3:3
- Romans 8:6–11
- 1 Corinthians 2:9–14
- 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14
- Titus 3:5–6

### Question 35 — What is righteousness?

**Answer:** It is God's goodness.

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 33:19
- Exodus 34:6
- Psalms 33:5
- Hosea 3:5
- Romans 11:22

### Question 36 — Can anyone be saved by his own righteousness?

**Answer:** No. No one is good enough for God.

*Proofs:*
- Proverbs 20:9
- Ecclesiastes 7:20
- Romans 3:10–23

## Salvation

### Question 37 — What is a covenant?

**Answer:** An agreement between two or more persons.

*Proofs:*
- For example 1 Samuel 18:3
- Matthew 26:14–15

### Question 38 — What is the covenant of grace?

**Answer:** The agreement God the Father made with Christ concerning His elect people, to save them from their sins.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 2:7–8
- Psalms 40:6–8
- Psalms 89:3–4
- John 6:37–39
- John 17:6
- Hebrews 13:20
- Titus 1:2
- 2 Timothy 1:9
- Ephesians 3:11

### Question 39 — What did Christ undertake in the covenant of grace?

**Answer:** To keep the whole Law for His people, and to suffer the punishment due to their sins.

*Proofs:*
- Romans 8:3–4
- Galatians 4:4–5
- Hebrews 9:14–15

### Question 40 — Did our Lord Jesus Christ ever sin?

**Answer:** No. He was holy, blameless, and undefiled.

*Proofs:*
- Luke 23:47
- Hebrews 7:26
- Hebrews 4:15
- 1 Peter 2:22
- 1 John 3:5

### Question 41 — How could the Son of God suffer?

**Answer:** Christ, the Son of God, took flesh and blood—that He might obey and suffer as a man.

*Proofs:*
- John 1:14
- Romans 8:3
- Galatians 4:4
- Philippians 2:7–8
- Hebrews 2:14, 17
- Hebrews 4:15

### Question 42 — What is meant by the atonement?

**Answer:** Christ satisfying divine justice, by His sufferings and death, in the place of sinners.

*Proofs:*
- Mark 10:45
- Acts 13:38–39
- Romans 3:24–26
- Romans 5:8–9
- 2 Corinthians 5:19–21
- Galatians 3:13
- 1 Peter 3:18

### Question 43 — What did God the Father undertake in the covenant of grace?

**Answer:** To justify and sanctify those for whom Christ should die.

*Proofs:*
- Romans 8:29–33
- Hebrews 10:9–10
- 1 Corinthians 1:8–9
- Philippians 1:6
- 1 Thessalonians 4:3, 7

### Question 44 — What is justification?

**Answer:** It is God regarding sinners as if they had never sinned.

*Proofs:*
- Zechariah 3:1–5
- Romans 3:24–26
- Romans 4:5
- Romans 8:33
- 2 Corinthians 5:21
- Hebrews 8:12

### Question 45 — What is sanctification?

**Answer:** It is God making sinners holy in heart and conduct.

*Proofs:*
- John 17:17
- Ephesians 2:10
- Ephesians 4:22–24
- Philippians 2:12–13
- 1 Thessalonians 5:23

### Question 46 — For whom did Christ obey and suffer?

**Answer:** For those whom the Father had given Him.

*Proofs:*
- Isaiah 53:8
- Matthew 1:21
- John 10:11, 15–16, 26–29
- John 17:9
- Hebrews 2:13

### Question 47 — What kind of life did Christ live on earth?

**Answer:** A life of perfect obedience to the Law of God.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 5:17
- Romans 10:4
- 1 Peter 2:21–22

### Question 48 — What kind of death did Christ die?

**Answer:** The painful and shameful death of the Cross.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 22
- Isaiah 53
- the Gospel records

### Question 49 — Who will be saved?

**Answer:** Only those who repent of sin and believe in Christ.

*Proofs:*
- Mark 1:15
- Luke 13:3, 5
- Acts 2:37–41
- Acts 16:30–31
- Acts 20:21
- Acts 26:20

### Question 50 — What is it to repent?

**Answer:** To be sorry for sin and to hate and forsake it, because it is displeasing to God.

*Proofs:*
- Luke 19:8–10
- Romans 6:1–2
- 2 Corinthians 7:9–11
- 1 Thessalonians 1:9–10

### Question 51 — What is it to believe in Christ?

**Answer:** To trust in Christ alone for salvation.

*Proofs:*
- John 14:6
- Acts 4:12
- 1 Timothy 2:5
- 1 John 5:11–12

### Question 52 — Can you repent and believe in Christ by your own power?

**Answer:** No. I can do nothing good without God's Holy Spirit.

*Proofs:*
- John 3:5–6
- John 6:44
- Romans 8:2, 5, 8–11
- 1 Corinthians 2:9–14
- Galatians 5:17–18
- Ephesians 2:4–6

### Question 53 — How can you receive the Holy Spirit?

**Answer:** God has told us that we must pray to Him for the Holy Spirit.

*Proofs:*
- Luke 11:9–13
- John 4:10
- John 16:24

### Question 54 — How were godly persons saved before the coming of Christ?

**Answer:** By believing in the Saviour to come.

*Proofs:*
- John 8:56
- Galatians 3:8–9
- 1 Corinthians 10:1–4
- Hebrews 9:15
- Hebrews 11:13

### Question 55 — How did they show their faith?

**Answer:** By offering sacrifices on God's altar.

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 24:3–8
- 1 Chronicles 29:20–25
- Hebrews 9:19–23
- Hebrews 10:1
- Hebrews 11:28

### Question 56 — What did these sacrifices represent?

**Answer:** Christ, the Lamb of God, Who was to die for sinners.

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 12:46 (compare John 19:36)
- Hebrews 9 and 10
- John 1:29
- 1 Corinthians 5:7
- 1 Peter 1:19

### Question 57 — What does Christ do for His people?

**Answer:** He does the work of a prophet, a priest, and a king.

*Proofs:*
- Hebrews 1:1–3
- Hebrews 5:5–10
- Revelation 1:5
- Matthew 13:57
- John 18:37

### Question 58 — Why is Christ a prophet?

**Answer:** Because He teaches us the will of God.

*Proofs:*
- Deuteronomy 18:15, 18
- John 1:18
- John 4:25
- John 14:23–24
- 1 John 5:20

### Question 59 — Why is Christ a priest?

**Answer:** Because He died for our sins and prays to God for us.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 110:4
- 1 Timothy 2:5–6
- Hebrews 4:14–16
- Hebrews 7:24–25
- 1 John 2:1–2

### Question 60 — Why is Christ a king?

**Answer:** Because He rules over us and defends us.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 2:6–9
- Matthew 28:18–20
- Ephesians 1:19–23
- Colossians 1:13, 18
- Revelation 15:3–4

### Question 61 — Why do you need Christ as a prophet?

**Answer:** Because I am ignorant.

*Proofs:*
- Job 11:7
- Matthew 11:25–27
- John 6:67–69
- John 17:25–26
- 1 Corinthians 2:14–16
- 2 Corinthians 4:3–6

### Question 62 — Why do you need Christ as a priest?

**Answer:** Because I am guilty.

*Proofs:*
- Proverbs 20:9
- Ecclesiastes 7:20
- Romans 3:19–23
- Hebrews 10:14, 27–28
- 1 John 1:8–9

### Question 63 — Why do you need Christ as a king?

**Answer:** Because I am weak and helpless.

*Proofs:*
- John 15:4–5
- 2 Corinthians 12:9
- Philippians 4:13
- Colossians 1:11
- Jude 24–25

## The Ten Commandments

### Question 64 — How many commandments did God give on Mount Sinai?

**Answer:** Ten commandments.

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:1–17
- Deuteronomy 5:1–22

### Question 65 — What are the Ten Commandments sometimes called?

**Answer:** God's moral Law.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 19:17–19
- Romans 2:14–15
- Romans 10:5

### Question 66 — What do the first four commandments teach?

**Answer:** Our duty to God.

*Proofs:*
- Deuteronomy 6:5–6
- Deuteronomy 10:12–13

### Question 67 — What do the last six commandments teach?

**Answer:** Our duty to our fellow men.

*Proofs:*
- Deuteronomy 10:19
- Micah 6:8
- compare Galatians 6:10

### Question 68 — What is the sum of the Ten Commandments?

**Answer:** To love God with all my heart, and my neighbour as myself.

*Proofs:*
- Deuteronomy 6:1–15
- Deuteronomy 11:1
- Matthew 22:35–40
- James 2:8

### Question 69 — Who is your neighbour?

**Answer:** All my fellow men are my neighbours.

*Proofs:*
- Luke 10:25–37
- Luke 6:35

### Question 70 — Is God pleased with those who love and obey Him?

**Answer:** Yes. He says, "I love them that love me."

*Proofs:*
- Proverbs 8:17
- Exodus 20:6

### Question 71 — Is God pleased with those who do not love and obey Him?

**Answer:** No. God is angry with the wicked every day.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 7:11
- Malachi 2:17
- Proverbs 6:16–19

### Question 72 — What is the First Commandment?

**Answer:** The First Commandment is, "Thou shalt have no other gods before me."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:3
- Deuteronomy 5:7

### Question 73 — What does the First Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To worship God only.

*Proofs:*
- Isaiah 45:5–6
- Matthew 4:10
- Revelation 22:8–9

### Question 74 — What is the Second Commandment?

**Answer:** The Second Commandment is, "Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of anything that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth: thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I, the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me; and showing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:4–6
- Deuteronomy 5:8–10

### Question 75 — What does the Second Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To worship God in the right way, and to avoid idolatry.

*Proofs:*
- Isaiah 44:9–20
- Isaiah 46:5–9
- John 4:23–24
- Acts 17:29

### Question 76 — What is the Third Commandment?

**Answer:** The Third Commandment is, "Thou shalt not take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:7
- Deuteronomy 5:11

### Question 77 — What does the Third Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To reverence God's name, Word, and works.

*Proofs:*
- Isaiah 8:13
- Psalms 29:2
- Psalms 138:2
- Revelation 15:3–4

### Question 78 — What is the Fourth Commandment?

**Answer:** The Fourth Commandment is, "Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: but the seventh day is the Sabbath of the LORD thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day, and hallowed it."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:8–11
- Exodus 23:12
- Deuteronomy 5:12–15

### Question 79 — What does the Fourth Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To keep the Sabbath holy.

*Proofs:*
- Leviticus 19:30
- Leviticus 23:3
- Isaiah 58:13–14

### Question 80 — What day of the week is the Christian Sabbath?

**Answer:** The first day of the week, called the Lord's Day.

*Proofs:*
- Acts 20:7
- Revelation 1:10

### Question 81 — Why is it called the Lord’s Day?

**Answer:** Because on that day Christ rose from the dead.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 28:1
- Mark 16:9
- Luke 24:1–6
- John 20:1

### Question 82 — How should the Sabbath be kept?

**Answer:** In prayer and praise, in hearing and reading God's Word, and in doing good to our fellow men.

*Proofs:*
- Isaiah 58:13–14
- Acts 20:7
- 1 Corinthians 16:2
- Luke 4:16
- Matthew 12:10–13

### Question 83 — What is the Fifth Commandment?

**Answer:** The Fifth Commandment is, "Honour thy father and thy mother that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:12
- Deuteronomy 5:16

### Question 84 — What does the Fifth Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To love and obey our parents.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 15:3–6
- Ephesians 6:1–3
- Colossians 3:20

### Question 85 — What is the Sixth Commandment?

**Answer:** The Sixth Commandment is, "Thou shalt not kill."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:13
- Deuteronomy 5:17

### Question 86 — What does the Sixth Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To avoid hatred.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 5:21–24
- 1 John 3:15

### Question 87 — What is the Seventh Commandment?

**Answer:** The Seventh Commandment is, "Thou shalt not commit adultery."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:14
- Deuteronomy 5:18

### Question 88 — What does the Seventh Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To be pure in heart, language, and conduct.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 5:27–28
- Ephesians 5:3, 5
- Philippians 4:8–9

### Question 89 — What is the Eighth Commandment?

**Answer:** The Eighth Commandment is, "Thou shalt not steal."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:15
- Deuteronomy 5:19

### Question 90 — What does the Eighth Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To be honest and not to take the things of others.

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 23:4
- Proverbs 21:6–7
- Ephesians 4:28

### Question 91 — What is the Ninth Commandment?

**Answer:** The Ninth Commandment is, "Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:16
- Deuteronomy 5:20

### Question 92 — What does the Ninth Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To tell the truth and not to speak evil of others.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 15:1–3
- Zechariah 8:16
- 1 Corinthians 13:6
- James 4:11

### Question 93 — What is the Tenth Commandment?

**Answer:** The Tenth Commandment is, "Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor anything that is thy neighbour's."

*Proofs:*
- Exodus 20:17
- Deuteronomy 5:21
- Romans 7:7

### Question 94 — What does the Tenth Commandment teach us?

**Answer:** To be content with what we have.

*Proofs:*
- Philippians 4:11
- 1 Timothy 6:6–8
- Hebrews 13:5

### Question 95 — Can any man keep these Ten Commandments perfectly?

**Answer:** No mere man, since the fall of Adam, ever did or can keep the Ten Commandments perfectly.

*Proofs:*
- Proverbs 20:9
- Ecclesiastes 7:20
- Romans 3:19–20
- James 2:10
- 1 John 1:8, 10

### Question 96 — Of what use are the Ten Commandments to us?

**Answer:** They teach us our duty, and show us our need of a Saviour.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Timothy 1:8–11
- Romans 3:20
- Galatians 3:24

## Prayer

### Question 97 — What is prayer?

**Answer:** Prayer is talking with God.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 17:22
- Genesis 18:33
- Nehemiah 1:4–11
- Nehemiah 2:4
- Matthew 6:6
- Romans 8:26–27

### Question 98 — In whose name should we pray?

**Answer:** Only in the name of Christ.

*Proofs:*
- John 14:13–14
- John 16:23–24
- Hebrews 4:14–16

### Question 99 — What has Christ given to teach us how to pray?

**Answer:** The Lord's Prayer.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:5–15
- Luke 11:1–13

### Question 100 — Can you repeat the Lord’s Prayer?

**Answer:** "Our Father which art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive them that trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, the power, and the glory, for ever and ever. Amen."

### Question 101 — How many petitions are there in the Lord’s Prayer?

**Answer:** Six.

### Question 102 — What is the first petition?

**Answer:** "Hallowed be thy name."

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:9
- Luke 11:2

### Question 103 — What do we pray for in the first petition?

**Answer:** That God's name may be honoured by us and all men.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 8:1
- Psalms 72:17–19
- Psalms 113:1–3
- Psalms 145:21
- Isaiah 8:13

### Question 104 — What is the second petition?

**Answer:** "Thy kingdom come."

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:10
- Luke 11:2

### Question 105 — What do we pray for in the second petition?

**Answer:** That the Gospel may be preached in all the world, and believed and obeyed by us and all men.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 28:19–20
- John 17:20–21
- Acts 8:12
- Acts 28:30–31
- 2 Thessalonians 3:1

### Question 106 — What is the third petition?

**Answer:** "Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven."

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:10
- Luke 11:2

### Question 107 — What do we pray for in the third petition?

**Answer:** That men on earth may serve God as the angels do in heaven.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 67
- Psalms 103:19–22
- John 9:31
- Revelation 4:11

### Question 108 — What is the fourth petition?

**Answer:** "Give us this day our daily bread."

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:11
- Luke 11:3

### Question 109 — What do we pray for in the fourth petition?

**Answer:** That God will give us all things needful for our bodies.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 145:15–16
- Proverbs 30:8–9
- 1 Timothy 4:4–5

### Question 110 — What is the fifth petition?

**Answer:** "And forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive them that trespass against us."

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:12
- Luke 11:4

### Question 111 — What do we pray for in the fifth petition?

**Answer:** That God will pardon our sins, and help us to forgive those who have sinned against us.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 51
- Matthew 5:23–24
- Matthew 18:21–35
- 1 John 4:20–21

### Question 112 — What is the sixth petition?

**Answer:** "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 6:13
- Luke 11:4

### Question 113 — What do we pray for in the sixth petition?

**Answer:** That God will keep us from sin.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Chronicles 4:10
- Psalms 119:11
- Matthew 26:41

## The Word, the Church, and the Ordinances

### Question 114 — How does the Holy Spirit bring us to salvation?

**Answer:** He uses the Bible, which is the Word of God.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Thessalonians 1:5–6
- 1 Thessalonians 2:13
- 2 Timothy 3:15–16
- James 1:18
- 1 Peter 1:22–23

### Question 115 — How can we know the Word of God?

**Answer:** We are commanded to hear, read, and search the Scriptures.

*Proofs:*
- 1 Peter 2:2
- Revelation 3:22
- Matthew 21:42
- Matthew 22:29
- 2 Timothy 3:14–17

### Question 116 — What is a Church?

**Answer:** An assembly of believers met together under the preaching of the Word of God.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 18:20
- Acts 2:42

### Question 117 — What two ordinances did Christ give to His Church?

**Answer:** Baptism and the Lord's Supper.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 28:19
- 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

### Question 118 — Why did Christ give these ordinances?

**Answer:** To show that His disciples belong to Him, and to remind them of what He has done for them.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 28:19
- 1 Corinthians 11:24–26

### Question 119 — What is baptism?

**Answer:** The dipping of believers into water, as a sign of their union with Christ in His death, burial, and resurrection.

*Proofs:*
- John 3:23
- Acts 2:41
- Acts 8:12, 35–38
- Colossians 2:12

### Question 120 — What is the purpose of baptism?

**Answer:** To show believers that God has cleansed them from their sins through Jesus Christ.

*Proofs:*
- Acts 22:16
- Colossians 2:11–14

### Question 121 — Who are to be baptized?

**Answer:** Only those who repent of their sins, and believe in Christ for salvation.

*Proofs:*
- Acts 2:37–41
- Acts 8:12
- Acts 18:8
- Acts 19:4–5

### Question 122 — Should babies be baptized?

**Answer:** No; because the Bible neither commands it, nor gives any example of it.

### Question 123 — What is the Lord’s Supper?

**Answer:** The eating of bread and drinking of wine to remember the sufferings and death of Christ.

*Proofs:*
- Mark 14:22–24
- 1 Corinthians 11:23–29

### Question 124 — What does the bread represent?

**Answer:** The body of Christ, broken for our sins.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 26:26
- 1 Corinthians 11:24

### Question 125 — What does the wine represent?

**Answer:** The blood of Christ, shed for our salvation.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 26:27–28
- 1 Corinthians 11:25

### Question 126 — Who should partake of the Lord’s Supper?

**Answer:** Only those who repent of their sins, believe in Christ for salvation, and love their fellow men.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 5:21–24
- 1 Corinthians 10:16–17
- 1 Corinthians 11:18, 20, 27–33
- 1 John 3:14–17
- 1 John 4:9–11

## Last Things

### Question 127 — Did Christ remain in the tomb after His crucifixion?

**Answer:** No. He rose from the tomb on the third day after His death.

*Proofs:*
- Luke 24:45–47
- 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

### Question 128 — Where is Christ now?

**Answer:** In heaven, seated at the right hand of God the Father.

*Proofs:*
- Romans 8:34
- Hebrews 1:3
- Hebrews 10:12
- Hebrews 12:2

### Question 129 — Will Christ come again?

**Answer:** Yes. At the last day He will come to judge the world.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 25:31–46
- 2 Thessalonians 1:7–10
- 2 Timothy 4:1

### Question 130 — What happens to men when they die?

**Answer:** The body returns to dust, and the soul goes into the world of spirits.

*Proofs:*
- Genesis 3:19
- Ecclesiastes 12:7
- 2 Corinthians 5:1–6

### Question 131 — Will the bodies of the dead be raised to life again?

**Answer:** Yes. "There shall be a resurrection of the dead, both of the just and unjust."

*Proofs:*
- Acts 24:14–15
- John 5:28–29
- Daniel 12:2

### Question 132 — What will happen to the wicked in the Day of Judgment?

**Answer:** They shall be cast into hell.

*Proofs:*
- Psalms 9:16–17
- Luke 12:5
- Revelation 20:12–15

### Question 133 — What is hell?

**Answer:** A place of dreadful and endless punishment.

*Proofs:*
- Matthew 25:46
- Mark 9:43–48
- Luke 16:19–31

### Question 134 — What will happen to the righteous in the Day of Judgment?

**Answer:** They shall live with Christ for ever, in a new heaven and a new earth.

*Proofs:*
- Isaiah 66:22
- 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17
- 2 Peter 3:10–13
- Revelation 21:1–4
